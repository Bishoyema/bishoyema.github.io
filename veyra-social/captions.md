## LinkedIn (single image, or attach `linkedin-carousel.pdf` as a document)

We built a portfolio website that scores 100 on every Google Lighthouse test. Here's what went into it. 👇

The project: a portfolio website for an AI creative and digital marketer in Dubai.
The brief: win a client in 10 seconds. Visitors should see the work playing, understand the offer at a glance and be able to reach out in one tap.

Why speed was non-negotiable:
Google's research found that 53% of mobile visits are abandoned when a page takes longer than 3 seconds to load. A beautiful site doesn't help if nobody waits for it.

What we built:
• A cinematic hero where short, silent film loops start on their own, and the full films load only when someone presses play
• 8 case-study pages with chapters and key frames
• One-tap WhatsApp and email, with the message pre-filled
• SEO, a sitemap and social link previews on every page
• Images in modern formats (AVIF/WebP) with fixed sizes, so nothing jumps while the page loads
• A static Next.js build, so there's no server to wait on

The result, on Google Lighthouse (desktop):
100 Performance · 100 Accessibility · 100 Best Practices · 100 SEO
→ 0.7s until the main content is on screen
→ Zero layout shift
→ 6 of 6 screen sizes tested, from 360px phones to 1920px desktops, with no broken layouts
On mobile, it also scores 100 for Accessibility, Best Practices and SEO.

The lesson: a website isn't a brochure. It's the first conversation with your client, and it has about three seconds to go well.

## Where the numbers come from

- **100 / 100 / 100 / 100, 0.7s, zero layout shift:** Google Lighthouse 12, desktop preset, run on the production build of the portfolio on 14 October 2026. 0.7s is the Largest Contentful Paint (when the main content is on screen); "zero layout shift" is a Cumulative Layout Shift of 0.
- **Mobile 100 Accessibility / Best Practices / SEO:** Lighthouse mobile preset, three runs, 100 in all three categories every time. Mobile Performance came out between 91 and 95 in this test environment, so the posts don't claim a mobile performance score.
- **6 of 6 screen sizes:** the home page checked at 360, 390, 820, 1366, 1440 and 1920px wide, with no horizontal scrolling at any of them.
- **53%:** Google / DoubleClick, "The Need for Mobile Speed" (2016): 53% of mobile site visits are abandoned if a page takes longer than 3 seconds to load.
- **8 case studies, films that play on their own, one-tap WhatsApp/email, SEO and link previews:** features of the portfolio site in this repository.
- **Reel, 100 SEO / Accessibility / Best Practices:** Google Lighthouse 12.8, desktop and mobile presets, run on the Oudelle concept site (`concepts/oudelle/`) on 17 October 2026; the reel's SEO section shows Oudelle. Its Performance score isn't shown because the test machine draws the 3D scene in software, which says nothing about a real phone or laptop.
